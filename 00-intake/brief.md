## Pivot notes — 2026-10-25

The founder changed direction from an add-on for established institutes / agencies to a tool Indian college students considering Germany use directly. Its confirmed outcome is study-abroad options matched to a student’s profile. The founder will reach out to students. Students can add as much or as little detail as they want, through both an expandable profile and a conversation. Both bachelor’s and master’s study levels remain in scope; the level should stay flexible.

The founder requested local HTML as the primary planning format. The workspace records confirmed inputs separately from open decisions and contains the initial research relevance review and proposed student experiments.

India → Germany, profile matching, both input modes, and flexible bachelor’s/master’s scope are founder-confirmed. The founder wants credible paths and cross-checkable proof, serving both unclear ideas and existing plans. Launch is free and founder-funded. Students or parents may pay later; ₹500 per month/plan is tentative and billing frequency remains open. Austria, Australia, USA, and Canada are later expansion ideas. Detailed student pain, the first subject area, target intake, and direct demand evidence remain open. Proposed matching behavior needs student validation. The previous agency pricing and Australia scope do not carry over. The Germany data architecture is relevant reference material; its proposed master’s-only data slice needs reassessment for both levels.
