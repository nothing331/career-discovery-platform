## Current direction — founder confirmed, 2026-10-25

- Serve Indian college students considering Germany directly instead of established institutes / study-abroad agencies.
- Deliver study-abroad options matched to the student’s profile.
- Support both bachelor’s and master’s options; keep the study level flexible and model requirements separately.
- Serve students with either a vague idea or an existing plan; aim for clearer, credible paths with source-backed proof they can cross-check.
- Launch totally free, with the founder bearing initial costs. Learn whether students use it and find it useful before considering pricing.
- Later payment may come from students or parents. ₹500 per month/plan is a tentative field; ₹1,000 was also mentioned, and billing frequency remains ambiguous.

- Founder will reach out to students to use the tool; exact outreach channel remains open.
- Students can provide as little or as much detail as they want, using both an expandable profile and a conversation.
- Present and update the product plan in a local HTML workspace in this repo.
